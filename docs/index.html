DDBX

Public director-deals research, with the filing first.

DDBX is a ChatGPT and Codex plugin for finding and explaining disclosed director dealings and insider share transactions.

What it does

It prioritizes primary disclosures—including SEC Forms 3, 4 and 5, PDMR announcements, SEDI filings, and ASX Appendix 3Y notices—and calls out plan sales, option exercises, gifts, tax withholding, and indirect ownership where disclosed.

DDBX is a research tool published by Jonathan Willington. It is not investment, legal, or tax advice. Always review the underlying filing before relying on a transaction summary.
